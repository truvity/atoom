# destination

The destination port: where a published document goes. A destination writes a document whole and atomically, removes it, lists what was written there with the digest of what it holds now, evicts the edge copy of public addresses, and reads a public address back the way a verifier would. As a ConnectRPC contract (`atoom.destination.v1alpha1.DestinationService`: `Put`, `Delete`, `List`, `Purge`, `ReadBack`), its stubs in Go and Rust, its documentation, and a conformance kit.

| | Go | Rust |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Port (contract and stubs) | module `github.com/truvity/atoom/destination`; stubs in `gen/atoom/destination/v1alpha1` (committed) and `gen/atoom/destination/v1alpha1/destinationv1alpha1connect` | crate `atoom-destination`: `atoom_destination::atoom::destination::v1alpha1` (generated by `build.rs`), and the Rust `Destination` trait with `Document`, `Error`, `check_address` and the other rules every driver and consumer shares (`std` only: no digest, no clap, no socket) |
| Conformance kit | the binary below runs against any driver | crate [`atoom-destination-conformance`](conformance/README.md): the suite, the ConnectRPC service and client of any `Destination` (the trait itself is the port crate's, re-exported here), the in-memory reference driver, `sha256_hex`; binaries `destination-conformance --socket PATH [--public-base URL] [--edge]` and `destination-memory` |
| Drivers | none in Go | [`atoom-destination-dir`](dir/README.md) (a local directory that a forwarder serves), and the reference driver `destination-memory` of the kit |

The contract is [proto/atoom/destination/v1alpha1/destination.proto](proto/atoom/destination/v1alpha1/destination.proto). Its rules in prose are in [docs/destination-port.md](docs/destination-port.md). Where the code came from is in [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md).

A Go consumer links a driver in process by mounting its `destinationv1alpha1connect` handler on an in-memory transport, or dials one that serves on a socket; a Rust consumer registers the driver's `router()` on its own server, or runs the thin main as a sidecar.

Dependencies of the port crate are the ConnectRPC and Protobuf runtimes and nothing else: the isolation check holds it to that, so a consumer that only needs the trait and the rules (an exporter) takes `atoom-destination` and none of the kit's `clap`, `aws-lc-rs`, `tokio` and socket host.

Status: v1alpha1. A field may change until the contract becomes v1beta1; a breaking change raises the minor version of the repository.
